English interview repair workbook

Manual practice · Six ten-minute pair drills

For candidates practising an interview in English

Repair the moment your answer gets unclear.

Choose one communication problem. Practise it with a partner for ten minutes. Leave with a clearer answer grounded in your own experience.

This free manual workbook focuses on conversational repairs: deciding what was asked, explaining responsibility, handling numbers, translating a tradeoff, acknowledging uncertainty, and knowing when to stop. It is suitable for independent practice and small peer groups. There is no account, score, upload, or live AI session.

Choose the problem you noticed

  1. 1. Clarify an ambiguous interview question

    Choose the question before choosing the answer.

    Ask one precise clarification, then answer the agreed question without apologising for your English.

  2. 2. Explain your work within a team result

    Make “I” and “we” refer to different, accurate things.

    Describe a team outcome while giving a listener an accurate map of your decisions, execution, and dependencies.

  3. 3. Explain quantities without inventing metrics

    Say what the number means, and what you cannot prove.

    Explain an observed quantity with its unit, source, comparison, and limits—or clearly say that you lack a reliable measurement.

  4. 4. Explain a technical tradeoff in plain English

    Translate the decision, including what the choice costs.

    Make a technical choice understandable to a listener who knows the business problem but not your tools.

  5. 5. Admit uncertainty and propose a useful check

    Separate what you know, suspect, and would verify.

    Answer a question beyond your current knowledge without bluffing, giving a specific next check and a decision it could inform.

  6. 6. Close an answer and invite a relevant follow-up

    Land the point, then leave room for the conversation.

    End an answer with a supported takeaway and a specific optional direction, without reopening the whole story.

How to use one session

Open a module that matches a problem in your last answer. Read its fictional example, then use your own true incident for the timed drill. Your partner listens for meaning rather than accent imitation. Write one repaired sentence and test it again with a differently worded question.

Every module stands alone and includes a goal, failure contrast, worked example, timed pair exercise, worksheet, and answer criteria. The worksheet accepts temporary text in your browser without sending it anywhere. Print from the browser menu for paper practice. Notes are not saved by the workbook; copy them before leaving.

Use it offline

Download the repository files together, then open index.html in a browser. Keep style.css beside the seven HTML files. All drills and worksheets work offline. Only the optional related reading links need an internet connection.

A good practice result is specific: your listener can repeat your meaning accurately and identify the boundary of your evidence. That result is useful preparation, not a guarantee about an employer’s judgement.
